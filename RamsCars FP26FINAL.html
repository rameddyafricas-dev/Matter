🛡️ Legal Checklist
Documentation, Ownership, History, Status and Physical Legal Standards
Title Document (Logbook / Original Registration): Verify the seller is the legal owner and the VIN/chassis number matches the document exactly.
Proof of Identity: Seller's government-issued ID matches the name on the title.
Roadworthiness Certificate (RWC): Current, valid inspection certificate available (or arranged as a condition of sale).
Service History: Stamped maintenance record showing regular upkeep and legal compliance.
Vehicle History (HPI Report): Checks for outstanding finance, recorded theft, or write-off/salvage history.
Licence Disc: Displayed, current, and legally valid.
VIN & Engine Number Consistency: Physical numbers stamped on the chassis and engine block match all paperwork with no signs of tampering.
Number Plates: Legally compliant plates matching the title, securely attached front and back.
